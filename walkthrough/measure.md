# Measure and save a screenshot

1. Press Measure, or M.
2. Click a point on the model, then a second point.

The distance appears on the line and in the status bar with its X, Y and Z parts, in model units. glTF files use metres and 3MF files use millimetres. STL, OBJ and PLY files have no unit, so the numbers are the raw coordinates. A third click starts a new measurement. Esc stops measuring.

Screenshot saves the view as you see it, with the grid, helpers and measurement, as a PNG next to the model or wherever you choose.
